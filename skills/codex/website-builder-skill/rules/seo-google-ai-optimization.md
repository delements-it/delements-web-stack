# SEO And Google AI-Search Rules

Google guidance: AI features in Search rely on normal SEO foundations. There are no extra special technical requirements for AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond being eligible for Google Search and snippets.

## Required Checks

- crawl/index allowed by robots and noindex controls
- title exists, is unique, and is meaningful
- meta description exists and matches page intent
- canonical exists for indexable pages
- HTML `lang` exists
- one clear H1
- heading hierarchy is logical
- important content is textual, not image-only
- internal links make important pages discoverable
- images use useful alt text or empty decorative alt
- Open Graph/Twitter preview metadata exists when relevant
- JSON-LD is valid and matches visible text
- sitemap exists when the project has many pages

## AI-Search Readiness

Check for:

- answerable section headings
- concise entity descriptions
- FAQ or comparison sections only when useful and truthful
- structured data that reflects visible content
- source/context links where appropriate
- up-to-date business/product/profile information

## Do Not Do

- do not add fake schema
- do not add hidden text for AI
- do not claim `llms.txt` is required for Google Search
- do not generate AI content without review for accuracy and usefulness
