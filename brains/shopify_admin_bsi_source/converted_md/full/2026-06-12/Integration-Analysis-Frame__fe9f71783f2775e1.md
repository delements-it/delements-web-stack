# Integration Analysis Frame

Khi Shopify tham gia trong workflow BSI, can phan tich:

- Shopify dang own du lieu nao
- he thong nao la source of truth thuc su
- du lieu nao duoc mirror read-only
- event nao trigger sync
- retry policy va reconciliation path

## Vi du

- Shopify to WMS inventory
- Shopify to Zoho orders or customers
- Shopify payment integration
- Shopify storefront plus CRM automation
